About Alien Quarantine

Alien Quarantine is a dark browser survival shooter set in a sci-fi danger zone. The atmosphere and confined spaces create a tense experience as you look for threats and manage each encounter. It is a good choice for players who want a short alien-themed action game without a large installation.

How to play Alien Quarantine

Read the opening prompts carefully, then use the supplied movement and aiming controls. Explore cautiously, watch the edges of the screen and do not rush into an unfamiliar area.
