How to play and improve

Drive through each district, avoid obstacles and use the vehicle weapons when enemies block the route.

Start with a controlled pace instead of chasing maximum speed immediately. Learn how the vehicle responds to acceleration, steering, jumps or slippery surfaces, then improve one section of the route at a time. In driving games, a clean line and a safe landing are usually more valuable than a risky shortcut.

Controls and first-session advice

Use the driving and weapon controls displayed in the game; watch the road before engaging enemies. On your first attempt, avoid changing too many things at once. Learn one control, complete one small objective and then build from that foundation. This approach makes the game easier to understand on desktop and, where supported by the embed, on touch devices.
