How to play and improve

Drag to aim, release the elemental barrage and use pickups to create stronger combinations.

Take a moment to read the situation before acting. Look for patterns, prioritise the immediate threat and avoid spending every resource at once. Small strategy games become more satisfying when you treat each attempt as information: notice what worked, identify the turning point and adjust on the next run.

Controls and first-session advice

Drag and release with mouse or touch input; read the board before committing a shot. On your first attempt, avoid changing too many things at once. Learn one control, complete one small objective and then build from that foundation. This approach makes the game easier to understand on desktop and, where supported by the embed, on touch devices.
