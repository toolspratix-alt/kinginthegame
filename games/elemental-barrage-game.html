About Elemental Barrage

A compact tactical shooter-puzzle in which monsters advance row by row. Aim elemental balls, use bounce angles and combine burn, freeze and chain-lightning upgrades to control the board.

How to play and improve

Drag to aim, release the elemental barrage and use pickups to create stronger combinations.

Controls and first-session advice

Drag and release with mouse or touch input; read the board before committing a shot.

Who is this game for?

Players who like short tactical puzzles with visible cause and effect.
