About WOMS World of Master Survivors

Defend a street corner against endless zombie waves; surviving earns upgrades between waves, including heavier ammunition, cover drones, turrets, and gunship calls.

How to play and improve

Defend the street corner from zombie waves and use the upgrades earned between waves to strengthen your run.

Quick tip: Use the upgrade choices earned between waves to strengthen your defense.

Controls and first-session advice

WASD / Arrow keys = Move; Mouse = Aim; Left click = Fire; Q = Deploy turret; E = Call air support; G = Throw grenade; P = Pause. Mobile: Left thumb stick = Move; Right button = Fire; tap the on-screen buttons for turret, air support, and grenade.

Who is this game for?

Players who enjoy top-down zombie survival and choosing upgrades between waves.
