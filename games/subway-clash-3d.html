About Subway Clash 3D

Subway Clash 3D is a browser-based 3D shooter set around a subway environment. The narrow routes create close encounters, so awareness and fast movement are just as important as aiming. Players can jump into the game directly from this page without installing a separate application.

How to play Subway Clash 3D

Follow the in-game controls to move around the subway map, aim and fire. Watch corners before advancing and use the environment to avoid standing in the open.
