About Realistic Tanks Poopy War

Realistic Tanks Poopy War is a tank battle game focused on vehicles, aiming and open-field combat. Choose your position, line up shots and use the terrain to make each attack more effective. The straightforward setup makes it easy to try when you want a browser game with a military vehicle theme.

How to play Realistic Tanks Poopy War

Use the game controls to drive, rotate the tank and fire. Keep moving when necessary, look for a clear angle and learn how the terrain affects your shots.
