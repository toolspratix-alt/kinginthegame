About Armour Clash

Armour Clash is a real-time strategy tank game in which the objective is to destroy the opponent’s base by deploying tanks. The listing says each tank has its own strengths and weaknesses.

How to play and improve

Deploy tanks by dragging or tapping cards and try to destroy the opponent’s base. Each tank has different strengths and weaknesses.

Quick tip: Choose tanks at the appropriate time, as the listing says selecting the right tanks at the right time is the best way to win.

Controls and first-session advice

On desktop and mobile, drag on cards to deploy units.
