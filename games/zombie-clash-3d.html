About Zombie Clash 3D

Zombie Clash 3D brings a zombie theme to a fast browser shooter with a colourful 3D presentation. Keep track of the space around you, manage each encounter and stay mobile as the level becomes more crowded. It offers an easy-to-start action experience for players who enjoy survival and shooting games.

How to play Zombie Clash 3D

Use the game’s movement and shooting controls to keep distance from enemies. Look around frequently, avoid getting surrounded and use clear sightlines whenever possible.
