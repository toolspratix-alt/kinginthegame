About Zombie Clash 3D

Join the Harlequins in a fight to reclaim an abandoned circus from zombies, using a double-barrelled shotgun or an explosive-arrow crossbow. The listing says victory can be achieved by sneaking the Scarecrow dummy from the Mad Circus and delivering it to the enemy base.
