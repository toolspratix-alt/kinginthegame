About Dead Zone Sniper

Dead Zone Sniper is a precision-focused browser shooter for players who prefer controlled aim over fast movement. Scan the scene, line up a clear shot and make every decision count as you work through the mission. The simple sniper format makes it easy to start a short session from any device.

How to play Dead Zone Sniper

Follow the in-game control prompts to aim through the scope and fire. Take a moment to identify the target and use accurate shots rather than shooting before you are ready.
