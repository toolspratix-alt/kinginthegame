Among Dungeon

Mini Strategy · Arcade Survival / Levels

PLAY IN YOUR BROWSER / NO DOWNLOAD · GAME HOSTED BY GAMEMONETIZE

Our editor’s take

A compact level-based survival platform game with hazards and a clear goal: make it from one side of each level to the other. It is suited to short, readable challenges.

How to play

Move through each level, avoid hazards and reach the exit while managing the timing of your steps.

Why we selected it

It adds a lightweight survival and route-planning experience to the strategy-adjacent collection.

Who it is for

Players looking for short platform survival challenges.
