About Monster Truck Off Road 2

Monster Truck Off Road 2 is a browser driving game built around steep routes, rough terrain and careful vehicle control. Choose your line through the off-road landscape, keep the truck balanced and push forward when the path becomes difficult. It is a good fit for players who enjoy short driving challenges and vehicle games that can be started instantly.

How to play Monster Truck Off Road 2

Use the game screen controls to accelerate, steer and manage the truck on uneven ground. Take your time on sharp climbs and descents instead of rushing every obstacle.
