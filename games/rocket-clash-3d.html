About Rocket Clash 3D

Rocket Clash 3D combines arena shooting with a bright 3D setting and quick multiplayer-style action. Move through the map, watch the angles around you and react quickly when opponents appear. Its compact matches make it suitable for players looking for an immediate action game in the browser.

How to play Rocket Clash 3D

Use the displayed keyboard and mouse controls to move, aim and shoot. Learn the map layout, keep moving between encounters and use cover whenever the arena gives you an opening.
