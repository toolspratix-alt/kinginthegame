About Battle of Orcs

Battle of Orcs is a strategy battle game built around fantasy units, timing and battlefield decisions. Instead of relying only on reflexes, players must consider where to commit their forces and how to respond to the opposing side. It offers a different pace from the shooter games in this collection.

How to play Battle of Orcs

Read the in-game instructions before sending units into battle. Watch the battlefield, choose the right moment to attack and adjust your plan when the opponent changes strategy.
