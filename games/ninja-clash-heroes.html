About Ninja Clash Heroes

Ninja Clash Heroes is a colourful team shooter with ninja characters, compact arenas and quick tactical encounters. Its character-focused presentation gives the game a light, approachable feel while the matches still reward movement and good positioning. It is available to play directly in a modern web browser.

How to play Ninja Clash Heroes

Use the on-screen instructions to learn movement, aiming and the available character actions. Stay aware of the arena, take useful positions and support your team during each encounter.
