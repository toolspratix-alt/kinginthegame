About Ninja Clash Heroes

Choose from a squad of heroes—including a ninja Scout, samurai, Buddhist monk, or sniper Geisha—and defend the Fortune Cat from enemies in an oriental garden. Unlock class powers, collect stars to open crates with weapons and boosters, and upgrade your character.

How to play and improve

Choose a hero and defend the Fortune Cat. Collect stars to open crates containing weapons and boosters, then upgrade your character.

Quick tip: Collect stars to open crates containing weapons and boosters.

Controls and first-session advice

WASD — movement; SPACE — jump; G — throw grenade; C — crouch; RMB — aim; TAB — score table.

Who is this game for?

Players who enjoy hero-based arena shooters and upgradeable abilities.
