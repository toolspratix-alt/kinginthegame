Provider status and publishing approach

Current catalog: The games currently listed on King in the Game are loaded from GameMonetize-hosted URLs. Other providers require their own domain approval, embed/API credentials and commercial terms before their games are added.

Publisher-ready approach

We use provider-hosted embeds or approved APIs rather than downloading, copying or re-uploading game files. Each title keeps a link to its platform listing. This keeps the portal lightweight and makes rights, source and removal requests easier to trace.

Possible distribution routes

  • GamePix: publisher/affiliate catalog integration, subject to approval and its SDK, advertising and content rules.
  • Playgama: partner widget, API or link integration, subject to written commercial terms and domain approval.
  • Gamezop: publisher onboarding through Business Alliances, followed by an assigned Unique Link, API or SDK.

Rights and advertising

Games are added only through a provider-approved publishing basis or a written commercial license. The portal shell does not add independent ad code or analytics. Provider advertising, cookies, SDKs and payment terms are controlled by the relevant provider.

For providers and rights holders

For a technical issue, partnership question or removal request, visit the contact page. For privacy details, read the Privacy Policy.
