Short version: King in the Game is a static browser-game portal. We do not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal information to play. Embedded games are hosted by GameMonetize and may be subject to its own cookies, advertising and privacy practices.

What we collect

The portal itself does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, passwords or payment details. Standard hosting and security logs may include technical information such as IP address, browser, device and requested URL. These logs are used for security, availability and basic diagnostics.

Third-party game content

Games are loaded from GameMonetize-hosted URLs in an embedded frame. GameMonetize and its advertising partners may process technical identifiers, cookies, advertising events and gameplay information according to their own policies. Please review the provider’s current privacy and advertising information before playing if you do not want third-party processing.

Local storage

The language selector may store your preference in your browser’s local storage. This preference stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Your choices

You can block third-party cookies, clear local storage or stop using an embedded game at any time through your browser settings. Browser and device controls may affect game functionality and advertising.

Copyright and removal requests

If you believe a game or image is displayed without proper authorization, please use the source link shown on the game page to contact the relevant platform or rights holder. You may also open a repository issue at the project issue tracker with the affected URL.
