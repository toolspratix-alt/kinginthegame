02 / QUICK PICK

A simple way to choose

  1. Choose the pace. Pick action for direct competition, strategy for more deliberate choices, or racing for a driving-focused session.
  2. Check the game page. Read its genre and short description before launching. Controls and device support can differ from game to game.
  3. Follow the on-screen prompts. Games are hosted through their respective platform embeds; use the instructions shown inside the game.

“No download” describes the portal experience. Individual games are supplied by third-party platforms, so availability, ads, and controls may vary.
