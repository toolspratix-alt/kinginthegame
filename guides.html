02 / QUICK PICK

A simple way to choose

  1. Choose the kind of session. Pick action for a direct contest, strategy for deliberate choices, racing for driving, or sports for a quick match.
  2. Check the game page. Read its description and genre before launching. Controls and device support can differ from game to game.
  3. Follow the in-game instructions. Games are supplied by third-party platforms, so availability and the play experience may vary.

“Play in your browser” describes the portal experience. Individual titles are hosted by their respective platforms; check each game page for its provider and instructions.
