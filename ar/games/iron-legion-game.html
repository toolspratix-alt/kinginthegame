Our complete editor guide to: Iron Legion

Iron Legion brings real-time tank combat into the King in the Game PvP lane. Choose a classic tank, read the terrain and work with your team in battles designed for up to 20 players. The mix of vehicle handling, armor decisions and coordinated fire gives each short session a clear tactical rhythm.

What makes the gameplay work

It is a strong fit for the portal because it combines PvP, multiplayer teamwork, military action and vehicle combat in one focused browser game. Tank choice, terrain and timing create a clear decision loop instead of a noisy game dump.

How to play and improve

Choose a tank that matches your approach, move with your team and use the terrain for cover. Light vehicles can scout and reposition quickly, while heavier tanks can hold lanes and absorb pressure. Watch the minimap, avoid exposing your side armor and push only when your team can support the move.

Controls and first-session advice

Use WASD or the arrow keys to move, the mouse to rotate the camera, the left mouse button to shoot and the right mouse button to aim. Start by learning movement and camera control before taking unnecessary fights.

Who should play

Players who want browser-based multiplayer PvP with tanks, team objectives, upgrade choices and readable real-time combat.
