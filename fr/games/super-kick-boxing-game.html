Our complete editor guide to: Super Kick Boxing

Super Kick Boxing brings a punchier combat-sport angle to the collection. The loop rewards reading an opponent, managing stamina and choosing the right moment for a counter, block or super kick.

What makes the gameplay work

How to play and improve

Stay patient during the opening exchange, block or dodge the first attack and answer inside the short counter window. Save stamina for the round’s decisive sequence and use the super kick when the opponent is exposed.

Controls and first-session advice

Desktop: Arrows or A/D move, Z punch, X kick, V super kick, C or Up block, S or Down dodge. P or Esc pauses. Mobile uses an on-screen pad.

Who should play

Players who like boxing games, timing-based combat and short arcade championship runs.
